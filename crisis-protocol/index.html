Crisis protocol

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page says what Lyveira does when something you write suggests you may be thinking about suicide or self-harm, what it never does, and what it records.

The short version
  • Lyveira is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number (911, 999, 000) first.
  • When a message suggests a crisis, the app shows a screen of crisis lines you can call or text with one tap, in place of an AI reply.
  • That screen is never behind sign-in, a subscription, a message limit or your AI setting.
  • Lyveira does not contact anyone for you, and no person at Lyveira is alerted.

What we check

When you write to the AI Coach, your phone checks the message for words that suggest a crisis before anything is sent. If it finds them, the message is not sent at all and the crisis screen opens.

Our server checks every coach message again, and it does so first: before it looks at whether you have allowed AI features, before it counts a free message, and before any limit. A message it catches is answered with the crisis screen, with no AI involved.

If you have allowed AI features, a second check by an AI model also reads each coach message for signs of a crisis that a word list would miss.

The same word check runs in other places where you write or search: your journal, Ask your journal, and search.

We do not publish the words or the rules, so that the check is not easy to step around.

What you see

A full-screen page with a short supportive message and these lines, each one tap to call or text:

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line (US): text HOME to 741741
  • Samaritans (UK and Ireland): call 116 123
  • Shout (UK): text SHOUT to 85258
  • Text About It (Ireland): text TALK to 50808
  • 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline (Canada): call or text 9-8-8
  • Kids Help Phone (Canada): text CONNECT to 686868
  • International Association for Suicide Prevention: a directory of crisis centres in other countries

You can open the same lines yourself at any time from the SOS button, whether or not you are signed in. Your Safety Plan is there too, if you have written one.

The coach does not answer that message with an AI reply, and it does not use up one of your free messages.

What we never do

  • Lyveira does not call emergency services, a crisis line, or anyone you know. Reaching a person is always your tap.
  • No person at Lyveira is alerted when a crisis screen appears, and nobody watches conversations as they happen.
  • Lyveira does not diagnose. The check looks for words and patterns. It can miss something serious, and it can appear when you are fine.
  • The crisis screen is never held back by sign-in, a subscription, a message limit or your AI setting.

What is recorded

If you are signed in, we record that a crisis screen was shown, when, and which kind of check raised it (for example, a word in a coach message). We do not record the words. This record is health-related information: it is part of your data export, and it is deleted when you delete your account.

If you are not signed in, we add one to a count and keep nothing else: no account, device or address.

A journal entry that our server flagged this way is left out when AI features summarise or quote your entries, so it is not repeated back to you beside something unrelated.

Reaching a person

The lines above are staffed by people who do this work. Lyveira is a self-care app and is not a substitute for them or for professional care.

For questions about this page: support@lyveira.app. That inbox is not monitored for emergencies.
