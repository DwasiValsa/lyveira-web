Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Lyveira (“Lyveira”, “we”, “us”) helps you support your everyday mental wellbeing. We care deeply about your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version
  • Your journal entries are encrypted on your device and again on our servers.
  • Your mood logs, journal entries, AI Coach conversations, and crisis-screen records are health-related information, and we treat them that way.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can export or permanently delete your data at any time.

Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address and a display name. You can also use the app as a guest, with data stored on your device.

Content you create

Moods, habits, journal entries, goals, safety plans, and similar content you choose to save. Journal entries are encrypted on your device and again on our servers.

Diagnostics

Crash reports, performance data, and basic product-interaction events that help us find bugs and improve the app. We minimize linking this to your identity.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, or precise location.

Health-related information

Your mood logs, journal entries, voice-journal transcripts, AI Coach conversations, safety plan, and crisis-screen records are health-related information. We collect them because they are the app — there is no version of Lyveira that supports your everyday wellbeing without holding what you chose to write down.

That information is encrypted in transit and at rest, and journal entries are encrypted on your device and again on our servers. We do not infer or record a diagnosis, and we do not use this information to decide anything about you. We never sell it, and we never share it with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, or employers. Lyveira does not read from Apple Health or Google Health Connect in this release.

How we use information

  • To provide and personalize the app (your dashboard, streaks, and insights).
  • To operate optional AI features that you choose to use (see below).
  • To process subscriptions.
  • To keep the app secure, diagnose problems, and improve it.

We rely on your consent, on our legitimate interest in operating and improving Lyveira, and on performing our agreement with you.

AI features

Lyveira’s AI features are optional. They are off until you turn them on, and we ask for your permission in the app before any of your content is sent for AI processing. You can withdraw that permission at any time in Settings → Privacy, which stops all further AI processing while leaving everything you have written in place.

Who receives the data. When an AI feature runs, the content it needs is sent to OpenAI (OpenAI, L.L.C., United States), which generates the response and the search embeddings. That request travels through Cloudflare’s AI Gateway (Cloudflare, Inc., United States), which routes and rate-limits it on our behalf. No other AI provider receives your content.

What is sent. Only what the feature you used needs:

  • AI Coach chat — the message you type, earlier messages in that conversation, and a short summary of your recent habits and mood.
  • Ask My Journal, journal reflections, correlations, and Story — the journal entries relevant to your request, and the question you asked.
  • Journal search and memory — your journal text, to create the numeric embeddings that make search work.
  • Journal prompts — your current mood rating and recent entry tags. Not your entry text.
  • Daily brief and reflections — your habit names, streak counts, and mood ratings for the last seven days.
  • Money and mood insights — the financial goals and amounts you have recorded, together with your mood ratings.

What is never sent. Your email address, name, account identifiers, password, payment details, and precise location are never included in an AI request.

How it is protected. OpenAI and Cloudflare process this data only as our processors, under written agreements that require confidentiality and security protections equivalent to those described in this policy, and that permit processing only on our instructions. Your content is not used to train their models, and it is not retained by them for their own purposes.

Even so, please do not enter information you would not want processed this way. The AI Coach is a wellbeing tool, not a medical or emergency service.

How information is shared

We share information only with service providers who help us run Lyveira, under contract, and each is required to provide the same or equivalent protection for your data as this policy describes:

  • Cloud hosting, database, and storage (Amazon Web Services) — United States.
  • AI inference and embeddings (OpenAI), routed via Cloudflare AI Gateway — United States. See AI features above.
  • Diagnostics and, only if you enable analytics, product-usage events (PostHog). Journal and coach content is never included.
  • Push notification delivery (Expo Application Services, which relays every notification on to Apple Push Notification service on iOS and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android). Expo receives your device’s push token and the text of the notification — which can contain a habit name you typed, or a line from your daily brief or your Story. It does not receive your journal entries, your coach conversations, your email address, or your account identifier.
  • Transactional email such as password resets (Resend).
  • Payments and subscriptions. Apple on iOS and Google on Android take the payment; we never see your card details. RevenueCat, our subscription manager, receives the store’s purchase receipt, your subscription status, and your Lyveira account identifier, so that what you bought reaches the right account — not your name, email address, or any of your content.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal data, except where we must keep limited records to comply with law.

One record outlives the deletion: a one-way hash of your email address, plus your email address and display name held in encrypted form, kept indefinitely so we can show that an account existed and was deleted and so the same address cannot be silently re-registered as though nothing had happened. It holds none of your content. Deleting your data lists everything we keep and for how long.

Your rights

At any time you can:

  • Access and export your data (Settings → Export My Data).
  • Correct your profile information.
  • Permanently delete your account and associated data (Settings → Privacy Settings → Delete all my data). You can also request deletion without the app.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have additional rights. Contact us to exercise them.

Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS), encryption at rest on our database and storage, field-level encryption of your journal entries and of what the AI Coach remembers about you, on-device encryption of journal content, and access controls. Your AI Coach conversation transcripts are not field-level encrypted — they are protected by that at-rest encryption and by access controls, not by a separate key. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

Children

Lyveira is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here and update the date above.
