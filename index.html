Questions, answered

Is Lyveira free?

Yes — the core app is free. Lyveira Premium is optional and unlocks the AI Coach, full mood analytics, unlimited habits, extra streak freezes, and more, with a 7-day free trial.

Is my data private?

Your journal is encrypted on your device. We never sell your data or show ads, and you can export or permanently delete everything at any time. See our Privacy Policy.

What does the AI Coach do with my entries?

When you ask it to reflect, that content is sent to our AI provider to generate a response. We don't allow it to be used to train models. The AI Coach is a wellbeing tool, not a medical or emergency service.

Is Lyveira therapy or medical advice?

No. Lyveira supports everyday self-care and reflection. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional care. If you may be in crisis, please reach out to a local emergency number or crisis line.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone first, with Android on the way. Join us at launch on the App Store.
