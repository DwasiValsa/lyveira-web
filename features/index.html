Lyveira's coach reflects your entries back with warmth and helps you reframe — and, uniquely, it remembers what you're going through and gently follows up. A short personal morning brief, an evening wind-down, and the odd delightful spark. It comes to you, caring, never naggy.

  • A personal morning brief and a gentle evening reflection
  • See — and forget — anything it remembers, anytime
