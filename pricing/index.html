The fine print, in plain words

How does the free trial work?

Premium starts with a 7-day free trial. You won't be charged until it ends, and you can cancel anytime before then in your Apple Account settings.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lyveira → Cancel. Your Premium features stay until the end of the current period.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your entries, habits, and garden — you simply lose access to Premium-only features until you resubscribe.

Is the free plan really free forever?

Yes. Mood, habits, journaling, and the Calm Corner essentials are free with no time limit and no ads.
